See whatmoves.

Orbitals is a time-aware sky explorer for NASA’s SPHEREx telescope. Pick a patch of sky, drag time, and watch asteroids slide across stars that hold still.

Starshold still.

SPHEREx revisits the same sky again and again. Each visit is one more frame of your patch, so fixed stars and galaxies stay put while anything that shifts position stands out.

Movers givethemselves away.

Subtract one epoch from another and a moving object leaves a bright and dark pair. Click it to open a dossier with its motion, its spectrum, and the evidence behind it.
